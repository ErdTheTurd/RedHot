    OPERATIONS BRIEFING

    • Account — Create a callsign (optional password). LOG OUT returns you to sign-in; from there you can log back in, use Forgot password (any callsign — device name mismatch does not matter), or create a new operator (replaces the local profile).
    • Commendations — Inventory → Commendations tracks career feats (wins, plants, crates, theaters, live multiplayer). Toasts fire when you unlock one.
    • Multiplayer — Same map & mode share one live match. You see other operators move, transform, shoot, plant/defuse, and drop mines when the relay is connected.
    • Catholic Trivia — Before DEPLOY, answer 5 questions (need 4 correct). Reloading and buying also ask a quick faith check; a correct answer when short on credits can grant alms. Type /no-questions to skip (type again to restore).
    • Matchmaking — Team modes search for live operators for 60 seconds. If others are on the site with the same map/mode, you share one lobby under real callsigns. Empty seats become AI.
    • Buy vote — When two or more humans are in a match, vote during buy phase to extend it (up to 2:00). Majority agreement applies.
    • Fleet crates — Tank, Ship, and Jet cases only drop that domain (plus Scout, Skiff, Wasp starters). Equip them in Inventory → Fleet. Arsenal only shows what you own.
    • Warheads case — Bullets, magazines, bombs (jets), torpedoes (ships), landmines, and more. Equip up to 4 in Inventory → Warheads; they load when a match starts.
    • Accessories case — Permanent mods: mine detector, tuned engines, plating, quick loader, extended mag, bomb bay, torpedo tubes, targeting scope, jump boosters, reactive shield.
    • Landmines — Plant with X. Hidden unless you are within ~3 units and stay still for 5 seconds (or own a Mine Detector). Your own mines stay visible.
    • Skins — Buy paint jobs separately in the Armory Skins tab, then equip in Inventory.
    • Ads — Short on bank? Watch a rewarded ad (max 5/day) when a buy fails for funds.
    • Maps — Four distinct theaters: Ironfront Harbor (river docks), Dustfall Mesa (canyon wadi), Frostbite Sound (ice shelves), Blacksite Yard (neon industrial moat).
    • Modes — Strike (plant/defuse). Skirmish (Lv 3). Siege Wave (Lv 6). Vigilante free-roam (Lv 10).
    • Vigilante — Solo sandbox. C opens arsenal (B drops bombs in jets). Esc to extract and cash out XP.
    • In-round credits — Buy tanks, jets, and ships during the buy phase (press B).
    • Bank — Match payouts go to your persistent bank (saved in this browser).
    • Armory — Spend bank credits on tank/ship/jet, warheads, and accessories crates + keys.
    • Inventory — Equip fleet, paints, warheads loadout, and view accessories.
    • Water — River and ocean are one continuous water body. Land vehicles can drive in, but they sink and take damage. Use bridges to cross. Ships stay in water only.
    • Graphics — Ultra is always the default. Check Low poly only if you want lighter meshes (saves GPU); uncheck to rebuild Ultra.
    • Controls — WASD/arrows (←/→ strafe) · Mouse look by default · Optional Roblox look on the menu (hold RMB to turn) · Shift+↑/↓ aim gun vertically · Shift+W/S jet altitude · F gun · B bombs (jets) / buy · T torpedo · X landmine · Space jump · R reload · E plant/defuse · Tab score · 1–3 slots (transformer morph between land/sea/air) · G smoke · V EMP · Enter chat (emojis) · /give-tokens · /give-xp · /no-questions · /lucky · /semi-lucky · /unlucky · /become-dev · /no-dev
