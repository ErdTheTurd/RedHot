OPERATIONS BRIEFING

  • Objective — Raiders plant the warhead at A or B. Sentinels defuse or eliminate the opposing fleet.
  • Economy — Earn credits each round. Win streaks, loss bonuses, and kills fund heavier vehicles.
  • Buy phase — Press B to open the arsenal. Sidearms → mid-tier → capital craft, just like a gun buy menu.
  • Controls — WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · R reload · E plant/defuse · Tab scoreboard · 1–3 switch vehicle slot
  • Classes — Tanks own land. Jets own sky. Ships own water. Cross domains carefully.
